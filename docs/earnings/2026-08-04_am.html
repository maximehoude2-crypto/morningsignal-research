Release-only read and operating engine

The release confirms the core toll-booth thesis: flight activity and OEM build rates are translating into demand across all three channels, while sole-source aftermarket pricing/mix sustains exceptional incremental economics. The tension is financial rather than competitive. TDG borrowed another $1.5bn, spent $2.2bn on Jet Parts/Victor Sierra, announced another $1.07bn acquisition, and repurchased $1.0bn of shares in the quarter. Those choices preserve per-share compounding but keep net leverage above the exact TIF threshold.
