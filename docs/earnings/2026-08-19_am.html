TJX — TJX Companies — PROVISIONAL - RELEASE ONLY

Decision line and subsector mechanics. TJX's off-price model converts branded-merchandise availability, merchant buying skill and rapid inventory turns into customer traffic, full-price-retail share gains and structurally high cash returns. Unlike a conventional apparel retailer, TJX buys close to need across thousands of vendors and can shift open-to-buy toward the best value; the moat is the buying network, store density and treasure-hunt frequency rather than owned-brand pricing. Q2 was operationally strong but did not clear the stock's priced bar: 4% consolidated comps and 11.9% adjusted pretax margin exceeded plan, while Marmaxx lagged the international and home banners and forward adjusted EPS remained below the more optimistic public hurdle. At $145.37, -3.6% premarket at 09:16 ET, the release supports the business but not an immediate multiple expansion. Action: HOLD; do not add until Marmaxx reaccelerates or the price falls below roughly $135.

The causal chain is branded supply and buying spread → compelling value and traffic → comp leverage and merchandise margin → pretax margin, partly offset by wage reinvestment. Public Q4 consensus was $3.55B revenue and $0.32 adjusted EPS. The preliminary FY27 frame was 3%-5% organic sales growth and 12.5%-13.0% adjusted operating margin. FY26 sales were $15.049B, +5% reported/+3% organic; gross margin expanded 150 bps to 75.5%; adjusted operating income rose 47% to $1.687B and adjusted margin improved to 11.2% from 8.0%; adjusted EPS rose to $2.51 from $1.51.

FY27 guidance is 3%-5% organic sales growth, 12.7%-13.5% adjusted operating margin—above the preliminary 12.5%-13.0% range—and $3.10-$3.35 adjusted EPS versus public consensus around $3.19. PRGP gross benefits target about $1.2B, a net reduction of roughly 10,000 positions, and a little more than $1.5-$1.7B in charges. Business delta: positive, especially China, fragrance and gross margin. Estimate delta: positive through the higher margin band.
