### ROUND 1: Independent deep analysis

#### CSX — rail operating leverage

- **Fundamental (9):** Q2 revenue +10%, operating income +17%, EPS +23% and operating margin +240 bp establish profitable growth.

- **Credit (7):** Approximately $18.1B debt is serviceable, but limits downside insulation versus net-cash logistics peers.
- **Sentiment (8):** Expectations are elevated after the run, yet the earnings beat is fundamental confirmation.
- **MomentumPulse (10):** Volume +6%, intermodal +9% and EPS +23% are simultaneous positive rate-of-change signals.
- **Freight & Network (9):** Network density converted six points of volume growth into 17% operating-income growth.

#### JBHT — intermodal recovery with operating leverage

- **Fundamental (9):** Q2 revenue +19%, operating income +32% and EPS +45%; intermodal operating income +58% is the core proof.
- **Quant (9):** +46.5% YTD and 28.5x forward earnings reflect strong revisions; valuation is high but not detached from growth.
- **Technical (10):** +3.9% versus 50-day and +30.4% versus 200-day; durable Stage 2 without the extreme short interest of RXO.
- **Macro (8):** Manufacturing and rail intermodal growth help; consumer and truckload pricing remain mixed.
- **Credit (8):** Balance-sheet risk is moderate relative to airlines and parcel incumbents.
- **Sentiment (8):** The recovery is recognized, but segment-level earnings proof reduces narrative risk.
- **MomentumPulse (10):** Intermodal +22% revenue/+58% operating income and DCS +9%/+9% are fresh confirmation.

Primary: [J.B. Hunt Q2 2026](https://investor.jbhunt.com/news/2026/07-15-2026-210601104).

#### UNP — high-quality railroad with merger overhang

- **Fundamental (9):** Q2 revenue +12% and adjusted EPS +13%, supported by freight revenue ex-fuel +4%.
- **Quant (8):** 21.8x forward P/E and +32.5% YTD are attractive versus slower parcel networks.
- **Technical (10):** +11.7% versus 50-day and +23.4% versus 200-day, close to a new high.

- **Sentiment (8):** Transcontinental-merger optionality is powerful but already a crowded narrative.
- **MomentumPulse (9):** Adjusted EPS +13%, velocity +5% and dwell improvement +7% support high-single-digit EPS guidance.
- **Freight & Network (10):** Best-in-class Western reach; a successful NSC combination could create end-to-end service but faces regulatory and execution hurdles.

Primary: [Union Pacific Q2 2026](https://investor.unionpacific.com/news-releases/news-release-details/union-pacific-reports-second-quarter-2026-results).

#### Next tier

- **ODFL:** Premium LTL density, pricing discipline and net cash deserve high quality scores; 35.9x forward earnings makes it an add-on-pullback, not a top-three new entry.
- **XPO:** Revenue growth and operating leverage remain strong, but 35.2x forward earnings and acquisition/operating execution create more downside asymmetry than JBHT.
- **NSC:** Strong network/tape with possible merger consideration; deal path and reported earnings softness keep it just below UNP.
- **DAL:** The best airline setup: Q3 adjusted margin guidance 11%–13%, EPS $2.00–$2.50 and full-year FCF $3B–$4B. Yet Q2 GAAP EPS fell 25% and fuel expense rose 67%, proving macro sensitivity.

- **MATX / SBLK / DAC:** Strong value and momentum, but spot-rate, geopolitical and vessel-cycle risks require smaller sizing.

### ROUND 2: ADVERSARIAL CROSS-EXAMINATION

#### CSX — peak tape or accelerating earnings?

**Bull (MomentumPulse):** Revenue +10%, operating income +17%, EPS +23%, margin +240 bp and intermodal +9% show broad, accelerating conversion.

**Bear (Sentiment/Credit):** +47% YTD and a fresh high leave little room for a service stumble; leverage reduces buyback flexibility.

**Resolution:** Bull wins. Paper BUY at $53.23; risk line $47.50. Q3 volume must remain positive and operating margin at least 37%.

#### JBHT — clean intermodal inflection or mixed-segment recovery?

**Bull (Freight):** Intermodal revenue +22% and operating income +58% prove rail conversion; DCS also grew revenue and profit 9%.

**Bear (Fundamental):** Integrated Capacity Solutions grew revenue 49% but gross margin fell to 12.5% and JBT moved to a loss.

**Resolution:** BUY, but the thesis requires intermodal operating-income growth above 20% and no further ICS gross-margin erosion.

#### UNP — network moat or merger risk?

**Bull (Network):** Velocity +5%, dwell +7%, revenue +12% and a transcontinental end-state create operating and service upside.

**Bear (Credit):** Approximately $29B net debt precedes a complex regulatory/integration path; reported operating ratio worsened on fuel.

**Resolution:** BUY with a smaller initial weight. The standalone railroad must sustain high-single-digit EPS growth; no merger synergy is required in the base case.

#### DAL — best airline or fuel trap?

**Bull (Fundamental):** Q3 margin and EPS guide plus $3B–$4B full-year FCF show pricing and premium demand.

**Bear (Macro):** GAAP EPS -25% and fuel expense +67% prove that revenue growth cannot fully offset the energy shock.

**Resolution:** BUY/monitor, below rails. Add only if Q3 operating margin reaches at least 11% and gross leverage remains near 2x.

#### UBER / RXO — growth narratives versus the tape

UBER's scalable network earns strong fundamental/network scores, but -20% YTD and weak technical/momentum readings prevent a BUY. RXO's +100% YTD and freight-cycle exposure score well quantitatively, but 26% short interest, a high multiple and integration risk create the highest broker dispersion. Both remain HOLD/monitor.

#### AAL / CAR / JBLU — cheap for a reason?

Headline forward multiples cannot offset debt, volatile cash conversion and weak competitive positions. CAR's public tape also contains an unresolved corporate-action normalization problem. All three are SELL/avoid rather than deep-value calls.
