- **Fundamental (9):** Fiscal Q4 EPS +66%, EBITDA +33%, inside same-store sales +5.5%, inside margin 42.4% and fuel gross profit +29% show multiple independent profit engines.
- **Quant (9):** +54.9% YTD, positive quality and earnings momentum; valuation at roughly 36.6x forward earnings is the constraint.
- **Technical (9):** Stage 2, +25.9% above the 200-day average. Support $790; upside objective $930.
- **Macro (8):** Rural/secondary-market traffic and prepared food are resilient, but fuel-margin normalization is the key macro sensitivity.
- **Credit (7):** Net debt near $2.4B is serviceable against $1.48B FY EBITDA; acquisition integration and lease-adjusted leverage matter.
- **Sentiment (8):** Crowded winner, but earnings validate the narrative.
- **MomentumPulse (9):** EPS, EBITDA, inside comps and gallons all accelerated into the most recent print.
- **Grocery/Wholesale (9):** Prepared food and private-label inside margin turn a fuel stop into a high-frequency food retailer. This is the best operating mechanism in the group.

### Key call

**Overweight operators that turn local traffic or independent cases into mix margin.** Do not treat all food retail as defensive: leverage and price competition make conventional grocery equities much more cyclical than their revenue.

CASY, USFD and PFGC rank ahead of COST/WMT because current earnings revisions and security-price asymmetry are better, not because their businesses have stronger absolute moats.
