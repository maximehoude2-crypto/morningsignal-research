## SUBSECTOR ENGINEERING PRIOR

Software economics are installed base × retention × expansion × price, less sales efficiency and compute cost. Data gravity, workflow depth, APIs, permissioning and compliance create switching costs. AI changes the unit from seat to outcome/consumption, making gross-margin durability and inference cost the new bottlenecks.
