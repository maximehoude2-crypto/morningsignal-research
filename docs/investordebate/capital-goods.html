### ROUND 2: ADVERSARIAL CROSS-EXAMINATION

#### WAB — durable rail flywheel or peak backlog?

**Bull (Cycle):** $30.93B multi-year backlog, 11.3% 12-month growth, margin +80 bp and EPS +21.6% show backlog converting with operating leverage.

**Bear (Sentiment):** +40% YTD and a fresh high capitalize execution; steel/freight inflation and international projects can defer cash.

**Resolution:** Bull wins. Risk line $280; Q3 must sustain >10% 12-month backlog growth and adjusted margin ≥21%.

#### RTX — broadest backlog or capital-intensive execution trap?

**Bull (Momentum):** Organic sales +16%, EPS +21%, $2.9B Q2 FCF and simultaneous sales/EPS/FCF raises are the strongest fresh revision package among mega-caps.

**Bear (Credit):** $30.6B net debt, Pratt durability obligations and capacity capex create long-tail cash risks.

**Resolution:** BUY; FCF guidance and Pratt margin are the falsifiers, not headline backlog.

#### GEV — power bottleneck or valuation bottleneck?

**Bull (Cycle):** Orders +88%, backlog +$13B sequential, 116 GW gas slots and $5.1B FCF prove scarcity monetization.

**Bear (Quant/Sentiment):** 41x forward earnings / 67x EV/EBITDA and +49% YTD require years of flawless delivery; wind remains operationally weaker.

**Resolution:** BUY but 1% maximum paper entry. Add only below $900 or after another >10% organic order quarter with ≥12% EBITDA margin.

#### POWL / FIX / EME — AI infrastructure alpha or capex crowding?

POWL has net cash and +98% YTD but now sits 28% below its high and 15% below 50-day, producing genuine score dispersion. FIX/EME have contractor labor/cost-to-complete risk but stronger backlog conversion. CIO prefers FIX, then EME, then POWL; all require disciplined entries.

#### TDG — franchise versus leverage

Fundamental/Cycle score the sole-source aftermarket at 9–10; Credit scores 3 because net debt is ~$28B. The existing TIF ledger controls: Q3 aftermarket organic growth ≥10%, margin toward 53% and leverage below 5.5x. At $1,236.71, HOLD; add trigger remains $1,150 with proof.

#### BA / HII / OTIS — avoid debate

BA's production recovery optionality cannot outrank negative enterprise earnings and ~$29B net debt. HII has defense demand but weak execution/tape. OTIS has service annuity but negative price/revision trends. None earns a short without catalyst; all are underweights.
