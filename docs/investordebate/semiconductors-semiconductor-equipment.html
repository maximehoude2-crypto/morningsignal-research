## SUBSECTOR ENGINEERING PRIOR

Value is set by architecture choice (GPU, ASIC, CPU), process node, advanced packaging, HBM bandwidth and WFE intensity. The binding constraints are leading-edge wafer capacity, CoWoS/advanced packaging, HBM stacks, interconnect and power. Fabless designers own architecture and software; foundries monetize process yield; equipment vendors monetize process complexity; test and metrology monetize defect intolerance.
